ABOUT US

The College of Informatics and Computing Sciences Is An Organization Dedicated To All Students from Information Technology and Computer Science Of Batangas State University - Alangilan Campus

Committed to advancing knowledge and fostering innovation at the intersection of information technology and computing sciences. Our overarching goal is to cultivate an academic environment that empowers students, faculty, and researchers.

Empowering Future Through

Informatics Excellence
Guided by these core values, we strive to cultivate a dynamic learning environment that fosters technological expertise, ethical leadership, and a collaborative spirit, preparing our students to excel in the ever-evolving landscape of informatics and computing.

OUR VISION

"To inspire leaders and innovators in computer sciences and technology, shaping a technologically advanced and socially responsible world."


OUR MISSION

"To providing cutting-edge education, fostering innovation, and nurturing ethical leaders who contribute meaningfully to the global technological landscape."


OUR GOALS

"Upholding Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, Collaboration, Diversity and Inclusion, Community Engagement, and Lifelong Learning among its Students."
