Association of Commited Computer Science Students

Overview

"Achieving Competence through Continuous Efficient Student Service"


The Association Of Committed Computer Science Students is an organization dedicated to all BSCS students of Batangas State University - Alangilan Campus.

Enriching students with essential tools for personal and professional development. They provide direction, focus, and motivation, helping individuals and organizations work towards desired outcomes.

Empowering Minds, Shaping Tomorrow.
