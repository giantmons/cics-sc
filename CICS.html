The College of Informatics and Computing Sciences

Overview

"Strive for greatness, EverUpward! ExcelCICSior!"


The College of Informatics and Computing Sciences Is An Organization Dedicated To All Students from Information Technology(IT) and Computer Science(CS) Of Batangas State University - Alangilan Campus.

To cultivate a dynamic and inclusive learning environment that fosters excellence in informatics and computing sciences. Our goal is to empower students with cutting-edge knowledge, critical thinking skills, and a passion for innovation.
