Academic Programs

Undergraduate Programs :

The Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) is a multidisciplinary undergraduate degree program designed to provide students with a comprehensive education in the diverse and dynamic field of information technology. This program integrates theoretical knowledge with practical skills, preparing students to meet the challenges of the rapidly evolving technological landscape.

Graduate Programs :

The Master of Information Technology (MIT) is a graduate-level program designed to provide students with advanced knowledge, skills, and expertise in the diverse and rapidly evolving field of information technology. The program typically combines theoretical understanding with practical applications, preparing students for leadership roles in the IT industry.
