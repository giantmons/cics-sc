JPCS

Junior Philippine Computer Society

Overview

"Empowering Young Minds, Forging Future Innovators"


Junior Philippine Computer Society - BatState-U Alangilan Chapter Is An Organization Dedicated To All CICS Students Of Batangas State University - Alangilan Campus.

To inspire, educate, and empower the next generation of tech enthusiasts by providing a supportive platform within the Junior Computer Society, where young minds can cultivate their
passion for technology, foster innovation, and develop the skills necessary to become the future leaders and innovators in the world of computing and beyond.

Fueling young minds with the power of technology to shape a brighter, more innovative future.
